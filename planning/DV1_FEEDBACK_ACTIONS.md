Retain the 15 planned charts and five sections. Changes are about composition, explanation and semantic consistency:

1. Use a serif title with a simple topic-appropriate character. Keep chart labels readable and body text plain.
2. Use one repeated two-column grid with consistent outer margins, one central gutter and shared chart/title/caption alignments. A full-width chart spans both columns; avoid extra narrow side-note columns.

Actual final notes need verified findings. The existing Brass #1/#6/#3 example is supported by the historical files and can be shown as a real local/global example.

Other charts use neutral marks/sequential grey scales with their own explicit legends. Names/labels must support colour, and colour meanings must be documented when implementing.
