Mechanic definitions

Mechanics describe how games are played; games can combine them.

Hand management: choosing which cards to keep or play.

Cooperative play: working towards a shared goal.

Drafting: choosing from available items.

Set collection: gathering matching groups of items.

Player powers: different abilities for different players.

Hexagon grid: movement or placement on six-sided spaces.

Simulation: modelling a real or imagined situation.
